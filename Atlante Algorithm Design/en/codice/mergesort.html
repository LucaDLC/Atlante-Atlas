Code: mergesort in Java

Divide and conquer in Java: recursion on indices, the merge with two fronts and copying the result back.

Divide and recurse

The function sorts the subinterval a[lo..hi). It divides in half and calls itself on the two halves.

  • The intervals are half-open: [lo, hi) includes lo and excludes hi. It is Java's convention for indices.

Merging

Two fronts, one per half. At each step I copy the smaller into the temporary array.

  • i++ inside an expression uses the value before the increment: here it moves the front forward by one.

Running it

Example of input and output (actually compiled and run).

8
5 2 4 7 1 3 2 6

(output)
1 2 2 3 4 5 6 7
