Code: interval scheduling in Java

Earliest finish time in Java: an array of intervals, a sort with a lambda, and the greedy scan.

Reading and sorting

Each interval is an array of two integers, {start, end}. I sort the array of arrays by the end.

  • Integer.compare(a, b) returns −1, 0 or 1. Do not use a − b: with large numbers it overflows and the comparison becomes wrong.
  • A lambda is an unnamed function passed as an argument: here it implements the Comparator interface.

The greedy scan

I go through the intervals in order of end. Once I take one, I update the end of the last one taken.

  • String.join(" ", scelti) joins the elements of a list with a separator.
  • "[" + c[0] + ",": the concatenation with + converts the ints to text.

Running it

Example of input and output (actually compiled and run).

6
0 3
2 5
4 7
1 8
6 9
8 10
