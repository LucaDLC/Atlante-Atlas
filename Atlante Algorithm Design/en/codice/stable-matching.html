Code: Gale-Shapley in Java

The proposal algorithm in Java: preferences as arrays, rank of the receivers, queue of the free proposers.

Preferences and rank

Each proposer has a list of preferences. Each receiver, instead, needs a fast question: which do I prefer between two? That is why I build the rank.

  • StringTokenizer reads the integers of a line; trim() removes the spaces at the edges of the line.

The proposal loop

While there is a free proposer, that proposer proposes to the next receiver on the list. The receiver decides whether to keep them.
