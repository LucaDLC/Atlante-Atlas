Code: weighted interval scheduling in Java

Jobs with values: sorting by end, p(j) with binary search, and the recurrence take or skip.

Sorting and finding p(j)

For each job I need the number of compatible jobs that come before it. After sorting by end, the answer is found with a binary search.

  • long for the values: the sum of the values can exceed int.

The recurrence

opt[j] is the maximum between taking job j (its value plus the best of the compatible ones) and skipping it.

  • job[j - 1][2]: the value is in the third column. The indices of opt start from 1 because opt[0] = 0 is the base case.

Running it

Example of input and output (actually compiled and run).

5
1 3 2
2 5 4
4 7 4
6 9 7
8 10 2

(output)
