Max-flow min-cut: the minimum cut

The max-flow min-cut theorem says that the value of the maximum flow equals the capacity of the minimum cut. The cut is read from the final residual network, and with disjoint paths it gives Menger's theorem.

The cut

A cut (A, B) divides the nodes into two sets, with s ∈ A and t ∈ B. Its capacity is the sum of the capacities of the arcs going from A to B. Every flow crosses the cut: so the value of the flow is at most the capacity of the cut.

The theorem

Max-flow min-cut: value of the maximum flow = capacity of the minimum cut. The cut is found at the end: A is the set of nodes reachable from s in the final residual network. The arcs leaving A are the cut.

Menger: disjoint paths

Menger: the maximum number of arc-disjoint s–t paths equals the minimum number of arcs to remove to detach t from s. For nodes (node-disjoint paths, or nodes to remove) the node-split gadget is used (see the gadgets page).

Project selection: choosing with the cut

You have projects, each with a profit pᵢ. The profit can be negative: it is a cost. Some projects require others: if you take i, you must also take j. You want the feasible set of maximum profit.

The trick: the minimum cut decides on which side each project lies. I build the network:

  • s → i with capacity pᵢ, if pᵢ > 0. If I leave the project out of the side of s, I pay pᵢ.
  • i → t with capacity −pᵢ, if pᵢ < 0. If I take it (on the side of s), I pay the cost.
  • i → j with capacity ∞, if i requires j. ∞ means "not cuttable": a finite cut cannot have i on the side of s and j on the other, so the requirements are respected.

Let C be the sum of the positive profits. For a cut (A, B) with s in A, I take the projects of A. The cost of the cut is C − profit(A): I pay the positive profits left out and the costs of those taken. So:

maximum profit = C − minimum cut

The Kattis problem Open-Pit Mining is exactly this: each block of rock is a project with a profit (value of the ore minus the digging cost), and to dig a block you must have dug those that cover it. The flow code is that of Ford-Fulkerson, with the matrix of residuals.

At the exam

"The minimum number of things to destroy to separate s from t" → min cut = max flow (Menger). Phrase: "By the max-flow min-cut theorem, the minimum cut value equals the value of a maximum flow. The min cut is the set of arcs leaving the nodes reachable from s in the final residual network."
