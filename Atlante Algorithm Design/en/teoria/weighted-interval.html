Weighted interval scheduling: DP on the intervals

When the jobs have different values, the greedy by end is not enough. One uses the DP on the intervals sorted by end: for each job, the best between taking it and skipping it.

The recurrence

Sort the jobs by end. For each job j, p(j) is the last job, in the order, compatible with j (it ends before its start). The recurrence: OPT(j) = max( v_j + OPT(p(j)), OPT(j − 1) ), with OPT(0) = 0.

Cost and reconstruction

Sorting costs O(n log n), p(j) is found with a binary search, then each OPT(j) is a comparison: total O(n log n). For the solution, go back: if v_j + OPT(p(j)) ≥ OPT(j − 1) the job j is taken, and you move to p(j); otherwise to j − 1.
