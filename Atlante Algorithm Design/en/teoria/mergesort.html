Mergesort: sorting by dividing

Mergesort is the template of every divide and conquer with linear recombination: divide in half, sort the halves, merge the two sorted lists.

The idea

Problem: sort n elements. Idea: divide in half, sort the two halves recursively, then merge the two sorted lists. If n ≤ 1 the array is already sorted.

The merge

Two lists already sorted are merged with two indices: you compare the first elements, you move the smaller into the output, and advance by one in its list. Each element is moved once only: the merge costs O(n).

The cost

Recurrence: T(n) = 2T(n/2) + O(n). The master theorem gives O(n log n). Intuitively: each level of the recursion does O(n) of work in total, and there are log n levels.
