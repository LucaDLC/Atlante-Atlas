Sequence alignment: edit distance

The edit distance between two strings counts the minimum number of insertions, deletions and substitutions. It is the DP on prefixes: a table of cells that depend on three neighbours.

The problem and the recurrence

OPT(i, j) is the distance between the first i characters of A and the first j of B. The base case is the zero row and column: OPT(i, 0) = i, OPT(0, j) = j. Then:

OPT(i, j) = min( OPT(i−1, j) + 1, OPT(i, j−1) + 1, OPT(i−1, j−1) + [A_i ≠ B_j] )

The three terms are: delete A_i, insert B_j, align the two characters (with substitution if they are different).

The cost

Table of (n + 1)(m + 1) cells, each in O(1): time and space O(nm). Reconstructing the alignment is done by climbing from the final cell towards the one that gave the minimum.
