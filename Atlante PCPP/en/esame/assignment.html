Assignments and oral feedback

The assignments are the ticket to the exam: 6 assignments, of which 5 approved are needed. They are handed in as a group on Monday at 07:59 and discussed orally with a TA. Here are the official rules and the status of your group.

The rules in brief

Groups
2–3 people (Group Selection on LearnIT)
What
each assignment = solutions to two sets of weekly exercises
Deadline
Monday at 07:59, before the lecture
How
link to the repo on github.itu.dk in the LearnIT form
Who hands in
one member for the whole group; the grade is individual
To be approved
all the correct mandatory exercises; the mandatory code must compile and run
Challenging
not needed for approval; needed for high grades
Tools
Java 25, Gradle 9.6.1

From the week 1 sheet: "Code that is close to correct, but that does not compile and run will not be accepted". It applies to the mandatory exercises.

Oral feedback

  • Slot of 40 minutes with a TA or a teacher, booked on the LearnIT Organizer. Every member signs up for the same slot. At most 5 people per slot: at most two groups.
  • The slot is recurring: same day and time in all the feedback weeks, 37, 39, 41, 44, 46, 49.
  • In person at the ITU. The TA books the room.
  • Keep the project ready to project or share: you are asked to explain and run part of the code.
  • All members must take part and be able to discuss all the exercises, not only those they wrote.
  • Review the answers shortly before: you wrote them days ago.

The feedback serves to "uncover misunderstandings" and to train "discussing course material". It is the same exercise as the final oral. You can also bring course topics that are not clear to you.
