Mistakes that cost the approval

Vague explanation

"The lock avoids the problems". It is missing what the critical section is, why two threads cannot be there together, which interleavings are excluded.

Proof by execution

"I ran it and it always prints 20 million". A test does not prove the absence of errors: reasoning is needed.

Terms swapped

data race ≠ race condition; synchronization action ≠ variable access; program order ≠ happens-before between threads.

Missing part

A mandatory exercise not done = assignment not approved, even if the rest is perfect.
