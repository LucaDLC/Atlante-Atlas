The two most common confusions

Race condition ≠ data race

A race condition concerns the result; a data race concerns the accesses. You can have one without the other: addIfAbsent on a synchronized list has a race condition but no data race (L4).

Thread-safe ≠ "seems to work"

In the course thread-safe has a precise definition: absence of data races on the fields. A test that finds no errors does not prove thread-safety (exercise 4.2.4).
