Map of the primitives

All the synchronization tools seen up to lecture 4, in one place: what they guarantee, which happens-before rule they give, and when to use them.

"Explain to me the various kinds of lock we used, I do not remember the various categories." This page is the answer, updated up to lecture 4.

Three families

1 · Lock

One thread at a time in the critical section: mutual exclusion. synchronized, ReentrantLock.

2 · Waiting for a condition

A thread waits for the state to change: monitor (lock + condition variable), Semaphore, CyclicBarrier.

3 · Visibility only

No mutual exclusion, only "whoever reads sees the last write": volatile, final.
