        How to read the atlas

        Every animation is a sheet: the steps are written one under the other, as on paper. Press Next → (or the arrows ← → after clicking the sheet). All steps shows them together for review.

        The colours always have the same meaning, on every page:

        arancio · la cosa che cambia adesso blu · il dato da cui parti viola · un secondo ruolo verde · il risultato rosso · scartato o sbagliato

        At the bottom of every page there is Mark this page as understood: the tick appears in the index and the count at the bottom left tells you how much is left.

        What you must be able to do at the end

        From the official course description, rewritten in plain words:

        • Design, test and run functional programs in Scala.
        • Use types to describe libraries: polymorphism, type-level functions, higher-order types.
        • Recognise monads (structures with map and flatMap), use them and design new ones.
        • Reason about eager (immediate) and lazy (when needed) evaluation: advantages and disadvantages.
        • Compare APIs in pure style and in stateful style.
        • Implement solutions starting from research papers (for example lenses).

        Estimated time: 50% homework, 20% preparation, 10% lectures, 10% exercises, 10% exam.
