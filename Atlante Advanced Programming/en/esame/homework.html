Homework: how to pass them

To be admitted to the exam you must pass 5 homework out of 10. A homework passes if it compiles and fails very few tests. Here: rules, deadlines, and how to read the test output to understand what to fix.

The rules

How many
about 13 exercise sets, 10 assessed, 5 passed are needed
First assessed
week 3 (Option), submitted in week 4
Who
individual: you may discuss, but you write the code yourself
What you submit
only Exercises.scala, no zip
Deadline
Tuesday 10:00, one week after the lecture
Resubmission
if it does not pass or you miss the deadline: by Sunday 23:59, two weeks after the deadline

1) The code compiles. 2) It fails at most 5 tests.

The week 1 slides say "at most 5 tests can fail". The LearnIT page of the course says "only six or less tests fail". Stick to the stricter rule (5) and, if you are at the limit, ask on the Discord.

The work cycle

Do not submit code that does not compile: the whole file would be worth zero, even if the other exercises were right. Leave ??? (it compiles, it fails only that test) or comment out the lines that do not compile.

"It worked before, now it does not"

  • Compile the whole file, not only the exercise you are working on. An error in exercise 9 also blocks the tests of exercise 1. Look at the first compiler error, not the last.
  • Dirty cache: scala-cli clean . and then scala-cli test . again.
  • Wrong imports: from week 3 the library lists are used, from week 4 the course's LazyList. The file already has the right imports: do not add others.

Asking for help

The course Discord (not email). You can paste code fragments into the question. The TAs give oral feedback in the exercise sessions, or written feedback on LearnIT to anyone who asks for it.
