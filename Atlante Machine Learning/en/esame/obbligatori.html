Compulsory activities

To be admitted to the oral four things are needed. Without them, the grade in the exam is NA and one attempt is lost. Source: the "About the course" page of the course website and the official description.

"Students have two attempts to pass each in-class test… Students who do not complete and pass an in-class test after both attempts are not eligible to take the exam." If you miss the first, the second is held one or two weeks later, outside the lecture time. It covers the material of the previous weeks: for the first, the pages of weeks 1–5 of this atlas.

If a student does not show up or has not really tried to solve the assigned exercise, the group is given another exercise to present (official description).

What is expected from the assignments

"Assignments are not expected to be flawless." They ask instead for:

  • An honest attempt: an honest attempt.
  • Reproducibility: a TA or a classmate must understand how you obtained the results (method, parameters, reasoning, code).
  • Reflection: interpreting and discussing the results, why a method worked or not, limits, alternatives.
  • Understanding of the underlying theory: how the theory connects to the problem, without copying the course material.

If an assignment is not handed in or is not sufficient, there is one more week to hand it in again. The assignments and the exercises are also exam material: the oral starts from there.

AI, collaboration, group

During the course

Generative AI is allowed as support for study, exercises and assignments. But you must be able to explain methods and results in your own words, because at the oral they will ask exactly about those.

At the exam

"No use of generative artificial intelligence is allowed at the exam under any circumstances."

  • Discussing and collaborating is encouraged. If someone has contributed substantially, one line in the notebook is enough ("Developed together with…").
  • All members of the group must take part and understand the work handed in. If a member does not contribute, it must be reported to the course coordinator.
  • Stuck for more than about an hour on an exercise? Ask the TAs.
