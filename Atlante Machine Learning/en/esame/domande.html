The 12 questions

Each question is linked to the exercises of one week (the folder WN) and has several focus points; at the exam one of them may come up at random. Here is the list from the exam document (July 2026 version, provisional until mid-December), briefly translated, with where to study it in the atlas.

"Exercise Week N" refers to the exercises in the folder WN, which mainly concern the lecture of the week before. The pages of the atlas are sorted by folder.
