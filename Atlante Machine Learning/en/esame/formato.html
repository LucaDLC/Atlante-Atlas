Exam

How the oral exam works

An individual oral of 30 minutes. You roll the die, one of the 12 questions comes out, you open with a presentation of 5–6 minutes, then there is discussion. You bring your notebooks, but the code is not run: what counts is how you explain the theory behind your exercises. Source: the course document "Exam Preparation" (July 2026, "tentative until mid-December").

How they assess you

CriterionWhat they want to see
Accuracyterminology, models and mathematical concepts used correctly
Understandingwhy a method works, how it connects to the theory, what role the formulas and the central assumptions have
Applicationconnecting the theory to the exercise, the data, the methods and the results; how it would be used in practice
Reflectioninterpretation, limits, uncertainty, model choices, alternatives: not only repeating definitions or graphs
GradeDescription in the exam document
02 (sufficient)substantially correct account of the topic; explains the main concepts and results, with some weaknesses
4–7in addition, clearly connects exercise, models and theory, with solid reasoning and interpretation
10–12precise, well organised, theoretically informed, critical; goes beyond description towards autonomous analysis and evaluation

"You are not assessed on your ability to recite implementation details or describe code line by line. Formal proofs are not required", but a clear intuition of the mathematical role of methods and concepts is needed. They also assess the ability to choose the most relevant parts of the programme for the question.

The 5–6 minute presentation

"We strongly recommend preparing for every exam question": a short presentation practised for each of the 12, and the recommended exercises used as a concrete starting point. The list with the links to the atlas: the 12 questions.

After the schedule is published (before Christmas) you can change slot only through a swap with another student, notifying Dan by email with everyone in copy. Illness or impediments: notify Dan as soon as possible. On the morning of the exam the updated time must be checked again.
