Descriptive statistics to assess models

Week 9, exercise Model selection and descriptive statistics, and the tutorial of week 7 on the Titanic dataset. Mean, variance, quartiles and box plot serve to describe the data. They also serve to describe the results of a model trained many times: how much it errs on average, how much it changes, how stable it is. Exam question Q7 (b).

"Explain how descriptive statistics can be used to assess model selection and the generalization ability of machine learning models." The heart: repeating the training on different subsets (as in cross-validation) and describing errors and parameters with mean, variance, correlation, box plot. Low mean = accurate model; low variance = stable model; training and test far apart = it does not generalise.
